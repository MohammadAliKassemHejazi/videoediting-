# Story comparison review

Four treatments share the original fictional script, English neural narration,
Lantern Valley character bible and 108.53-second timeline. Each exported film is
1440 × 2560 at 60fps and passes a full decode check. All four also loaded and
started playback in Microsoft Edge through the local gallery without a media error.

Inspected eight phone frames per treatment, including the bridge inspection,
repair, load test, crossing and lighthouse payoff. Captions remain legible at phone
size. The final illustrated and sketch exports remove the inspection annotation
that previously competed with captions. Characters preserve their silhouettes,
relative sizes, antenna counts and identifying accessories across treatments.
The sketch uses ink outlines and direct colors, avoiding the expensive per-shape
grayscale filter. The cinematic inspection shots deliberately prioritize the
bridge; the wider payoff reveals the connected village and lighthouse.

These are direction drafts. A listening review has not been recorded. The stylized
procedural characters and environments establish this particular sample story;
they are not a substitute for directing fresh acting, locations and assets from
each future script. Choose a treatment in the gallery, then assess its full motion,
narration and pacing before approving a production film.
