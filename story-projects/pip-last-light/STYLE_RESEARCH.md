# Story research — 2026-10-13

- YouTube Culture & Trends, April 9 2026, Animation's New Wave:
  https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/independent-animation-trends-report/
  Reports substantial interest in independent animation among surveyed 14–24-year-old
  animation fans. This supports testing original recurring characters and series;
  it does not measure an exact 13–17 audience or guarantee engagement.
- YouTube Culture & Trends, September 10 2026, modern mainstream:
  https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/youtube-culture-trends-modern-mainstream-research/
  Discusses community participation and story/lore exploration. Applied direction:
  a consistent world, meaningful clues and an earned episode outcome.
- TikTok Next 2026, January 14:
  https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-tiktok-next-2026-our-trend-forecast-for-marketers-for-the-year-ahead?lang=en
  Broad platform forecast emphasizing curiosity and emotional value. Applied
  direction: a visible question in the opening, discovery and a resolved payoff.

Creative inference for ages 13–17: test short mystery/adventure, character-driven
mini-series, sketch storytime, and educational challenges with visible consequences.
Use original characters and scripts. Do not copy an existing franchise or present
these directions as measured viral rankings. Recheck recent examples per new topic.
