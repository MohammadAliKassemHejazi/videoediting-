# A path you can trust

Audience: ages 13–17. Original fictional sample; curious adventure and a problem
worth solving. Avoid a preschool narrator, chapter-card slideshow or repeated
moral statements. A light capsule, a visible gap and an earned crossing carry the
story. Fast hook, visible stakes, reversal, investigation, repair, two tests,
brief stillness before ignition, then a complete payoff with room for a sequel.

Four treatments: tactile educational 3D, illustrated character adventure, cinematic
3D coverage and ink-sketch adventure. Cameras reveal causes and reactions. Actors
anticipate, look, move, make contact and react. Full-screen scenes, 2K at 60fps.
Narration selectable English or Arabic; the sample is English. The locked bible
supplies Pip and Ada. The scene module directs fresh actions, not a universal plot.

Professional revision: rounded geometry, free CC0 lighting/wood, soft contact
shadows, a moving river, within-scene coverage, facial reactions, tracked 2D camera,
fewer on-screen headings, phrase-aware captions and causal foley. Judge actual
rendered frames and transitions; these are drafts for creative selection.
