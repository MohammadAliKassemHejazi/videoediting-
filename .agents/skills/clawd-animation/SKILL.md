---
name: clawd-animation
description: Create hand-drawn character animations and narrative explainers using the downloaded Clawd Animation starter kit, adapted to this studio's preview and export workflow.
---

Read [the original animation guide](upstream/ANIMATION_GUIDE.md) and inspect the
model sheets in `upstream/docs/` before designing. The full upstream starter kit,
character engine and renderer are downloaded in `upstream/`; preserve its license.
Use original or user-specified characters. Act out intention, obstacle, reaction
and resolution; use anticipation, weight and gaze to make actions readable.

For this user's workflow, read [the story workflow](../../../docs/STORY_STUDIO.md).
Open Remotion while working. Use full-frame storytelling, minimum 60-second story
drafts, 1440×2560 at 60fps, and selectable English/Arabic generated narration.
Preserve recurring characters through `series/<id>/bible.json`, model sheets and
immutable IDs. The user's captions, duration, preview and permission choices take
precedence over upstream defaults. Do not install p5 dependencies unless needed for
the chosen treatment. Code-drawn previews are allowed, but identify them accurately.
