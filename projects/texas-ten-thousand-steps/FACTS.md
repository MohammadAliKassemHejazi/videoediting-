# Primary fact sheet — checked2026-10-26

- Yamasa's own product history dates the first consumer Manpo Meter to1965 and
  says development began following a1963 request from a walking campaign linked
  to doctors/journalists. Its account explicitly disputes “only a catchy number”
  as the whole explanation. Treat the walking-man-symbol origin as a popular
  account, not established manufacturer intent.
  https://www.yamasa-tokei.co.jp/top_category/history2.html
- Lee et al.,2019, JAMA Internal Medicine: observational study of older women,
  with lower mortality associated with counts below10,000. Do not generalize
  the age-specific findings into a prescription for teenagers or prove causation.
  https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamainternalmedicine/fullarticle/2734709
- Ding et al.,2025, Lancet Public Health: systematic review57 studies; adult
  evidence, non-linear associations for some outcomes and linear for others.
  Seven thousand can be a practical marker for some;10,000 remains viable for
  more active people. Residual confounding and limited age-specific analysis
  matter. Do not say all benefit stops at7000 or that10,000 is harmful/useless.
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40713949/
- Paluch et al.,2022: adult-cohort synthesis supports different associations
  by age, further evidence against treating one number as a universal switch.
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35247352/
