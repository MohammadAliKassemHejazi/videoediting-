# My Shadow Went First

Original Arabic story for ages 13–17. A dancer's shadow leaves her recording to
lead her to a classmate's forgotten birthday. Curiosity becomes accountability,
then shared play. The final shadow/cake gag returns the mystery without undoing
the emotional resolution. No view-count claims inside the film.

Visual treatment: ink-black night, teal/lavender clothing, warm classroom light,
articulated characters, expressive brows/gaze, paper rockets and hard cuts on
action. Phone composition gives way to real face-to-face staging. Full portrait
1440×2560 at 60fps, at least 60 seconds, free neural Arabic narration. Caption
overlays support the acting; they do not reserve a lower band.

Series identity lives in series/noor-shadow/bible.json. Exact scene timings follow
the generated narration, not a fabricated reading estimate.
