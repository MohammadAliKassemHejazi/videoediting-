One continuous cinematic animated shot. The copper fox and little silver cream kitten sit together on the wooden tram-stop bench. The kitten leans her head against the fox's shoulder, looks up at his face and smiles. The fox looks from the departing blue-green tram behind them back down to the kitten and smiles warmly, choosing to wait with her. Their dangling paws swing a little and settle naturally; their bodies remain seated on the bench. The tram slowly rolls out of frame to the right, revealing blossom trees in the background. Constant soft golden hour light and fine fur, lavender scarf, green woven satchel, one stable tail per character. Fixed camera, no cut, no new characters. Peaceful held ending after the decision.
