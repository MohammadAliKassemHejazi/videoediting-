# Original 15-second microstory / The Next Tram

Use `series/ember-luma/bible.json` and the original keyframe for every shot. These
are production prompts, not completed clips. Each shot changes the story.

0–3s: Medium two-shot at kitten eye height. Ember places one foot on the tram step,
looks towards the open blue-green tram door and adjusts his moss satchel. Luma
looks at his face, then gently reaches for his charcoal forepaw. Fixed camera;
read the separation before contact. One tail per character, stable fur markings.

3–6s: Same camera side, closer view of paws and faces. Luma holds Ember's forepaw
with both cream paws. Ember stops, glances down at the contact, then softens his
eyebrows. Hold the contact naturally; no interpenetration, rubber limbs or extra paws.

6–10s: Medium two-shot. Ember steps back down onto the pavement and kneels to
Luma's height; his satchel settles on his hip. Luma releases the paw and leans into
a gentle shoulder hug. Their planted feet remain on the pavement. Warm backlight
shows fine fur; soft sky fill preserves eyes. Camera pushes only as the hug lands.

10–13s: Reaction close-up. Ember opens one eye at the tram bell, then looks back
at Luma and smiles. Luma notices the bell but does not tighten the hug. Read each
reaction separately; do not cut before the face changes.

13–15s: Wider shot. The tram doors close behind them. Ember sits beside Luma on
the bench, both looking towards the track, tails settling. A small affectionate
lean is the payoff: he chooses the next tram. Leave a clean final hold.

Negative constraints: no text baked into the scene, no watermark, no changed
accessories, no new fur colors, no duplicate tails, no foot sliding, no merged paws,
no face morphing, no sudden camera reversal. Add narration/captions in the studio.
