# Noir clockwork cinema

Palette: ink-black architecture, cold satin chrome, rare deep red strips, amber
time energy. Native 3D materials and practical light define the frame; UI graphics
and repeated title cards do not belong to this style.

Camera vocabulary: intimate material macro, chase, narrow-aperture passage,
impact cut, wide release. Move to reveal a cause or consequence. Preserve metal
detail beneath bloom. A foreground obstacle must never hide the decisive action.

Sound vocabulary: inharmonic clock ticks, metallic wings, filtered room air,
mechanical contacts, a short silence before release. The ending resolves the
object's material sound rather than applying a stock chime.

Reuse the style, not this plot. Author a new world rule and shot list for a new
script. Related episodes preserve `series/midnight-couriers/bible.json`.
