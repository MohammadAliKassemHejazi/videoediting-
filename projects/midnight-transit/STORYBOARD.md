# MIDNIGHT TRANSIT: director's timing sheet

- **0–3s:** Macro clock mechanism; one amber second wakes the moth.
  Sound: Dry ticks; tiny metal latch; descending air.

- **3–6s:** Camera follows the moth down a red-lit industrial corridor.
  Sound: Panned wing-metal flutter; room rumble.

- **6–9s:** Clock blades close; moth folds its wings and dives through.
  Sound: Mechanical slam; impact and short ringing tail.

- **9–12s:** Time capsule strikes the clock core; rings burst outward.
  Sound: Brief hold, then glass burst and sub impact.

- **12–15s:** Released amber light travels through the city; moth flies toward the sunrise.
  Sound: Warm resolving chord; distant metal tail.
