# Kinetic brutalist print

Palette: chartreuse #d9ff43, black #17191a, ivory #f1eee6, violet #7643ed.
Native SVG is the editable source. Huge type and optical geometry create the
composition; no generic rounded cards or character animation.

Motion grammar: noise collision, pointer contact, compression, a word landing,
radial expansion and a final print lock. Typography changes hierarchy and
occupies the frame. The headline receives a clean read while the machine works
below it. Deliberate outer-edge crops may affect decorative forms, never the
essential message. Final holds support reading rather than idle decoration.

Sound is dry, percussive and graphic: contact clicks, a rising air pull, print
impacts, ratchet-like metallic taps and a single final bass hit. New scripts get
new contact times and words, not a renamed version of the NOISE/IDEA poster.
