# SIGNAL / NOISE

Original 15-second, 2K/60fps style film. No supplied footage.

A field of noise is physically crushed into one idea, then printed into an explosive poster.

Style: Kinetic brutalist print lab. Palette: #d9ff43, #17191a, #f1eee6, #7643ed.

Skills: hyperframes-animation / kinetic typography; hyperframes-creative / video composition; onetake / contact and carrier; lemo-opuscar / swiss-motion; oversized-cursor / causation. Their creative techniques are adapted to the existing Remotion runtime; upstream paid APIs and installers are unused.

The other two films have different plots, media, cameras, typography, colors and scores. No shared scene painter, UI card template, character rig or story.
