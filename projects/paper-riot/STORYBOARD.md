# PAPER RIOT: director's timing sheet

- **0–3s:** Pencil drops; Nib dodges the drawing stroke with a huge facial take.
  Sound: Graphite scrape; paper slap; comic knock.

- **3–6s:** The pencil draws a door; Nib runs for it. The door grows teeth.
  Sound: Drawing rasp; rushing footsteps; wood bite.

- **6–9s:** Nib yanks the edge of the page and rips a huge diagonal tear.
  Sound: Long paper rip, then breath of quiet.

- **9–12s:** The torn page folds into two wings. Nib springs up.
  Sound: Two fold snaps; lift whoosh.

- **12–15s:** The creature escapes its own page; paper confetti settles around a triumph pose.
  Sound: Flutter, bright percussion and a soft final flap.

Render only after inspecting phone frames and the affected seams. No claim of listening review from meters.
