# PAPER RIOT

Original 15-second, 2K/60fps style film. No supplied footage.

A giant pencil draws a trap. An ink creature turns the trap into wings.

Style: Ink cartoon × editorial collage. Palette: #f3ebd5, #2433a8, #ef542f.

Skills: clawd-animation / acting and line boil; gbro-collage-broll / tactile cutouts; lemo-opuscar / paper-popup; cut-the-curve / carrier transitions. Their creative techniques are adapted to the existing Remotion runtime; upstream paid APIs and installers are unused.

The other two films have different plots, media, cameras, typography, colors and scores. No shared scene painter, UI card template, character rig or story.
