# Ink cartoon × editorial collage

Palette: warm notebook stock, indigo ink protagonist, orange photographic pencil,
ivory cut edges. Cutouts have tangible edges and shadows; ink has a fixed identity
and deterministic twelve-drawing-per-second line boil.

Acting: anticipate, make contact, react, hold for the read, then follow through.
Use clear eye direction, bent limbs, squash and a large facial take. Keep the
creature large enough for the phone frame. A drawn object must do something.

Transitions belong to the medium: drawing, tearing, folding, page escape. The
camera can follow action in the flat world; it does not fake a spinning 3D mascot.
The photographic prop and illustrated character remain visibly different media.

Sound: pencil rasp, paper slap, footsteps, a threatening bite, long tear, two
folds and wing flutter. Leave room around the tear's resolution and the take.

Related films preserve `series/paper-riot/bible.json`. Fresh stories require new
props, situations and actions instead of always reusing the door/wings story.
