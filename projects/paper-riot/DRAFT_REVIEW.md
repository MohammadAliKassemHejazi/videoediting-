and varied its camera framing. The photographic pencil, drawn trap, surprise take,
page tear, wing fold and escape have distinct reads. Checked the title, face and
paper edge at phone size. This film has its own canvas painter and cutout asset;
it does not reuse the prior robot story.

levels were measured; that is not a listening review. This remains a direction
