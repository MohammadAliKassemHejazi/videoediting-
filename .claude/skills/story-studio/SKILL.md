---
name: story-studio
description: Turn a supplied story or educational script into a narrated character video of at least one minute, with English or Arabic voiceover and consistent characters across related episodes.
---

Read [the story studio workflow](../../../docs/STORY_STUDIO.md). The user supplies
the script; the agent directs the film. Preserve the script's meaning and facts,
write a real narrative treatment and timed storyboard, create character assets,
generate voiceover and render a reviewable draft. Do not turn every paragraph into
a title card. Education uses demonstrations; stories show characters making choices
that visibly cause consequences. Read the relevant installed motion/character skill.

Open Remotion before working and leave it visible. Offer educational 3D,
illustrated character storytelling and cinematic story animation alongside the
five motion families, plus ink-sketch stories. Default story audience: ages 13–17.
Research current independent animation and story formats; direct original characters
and situations. Use expressive acting, purposeful camera coverage, clear contact,
material/light quality and causal sound. Continuous development must reveal the
story; decorative drift is insufficient. Render selected story drafts in full, minimum 60 seconds,
at 1440×2560/60fps. Landscape 2K needs an intentional 2560×1440 reframe.

For a related story, load the existing `series/<id>/bible.json`, prior episode
summary and character models before writing. Keep identity IDs, geometry, palette,
costume, proportions and established facts stable. Record new continuity facts as
an episode outcome; never overwrite the bible to fit a new scene. Preserve any
user-supplied voices, assets or references. Do not claim procedural models are
photorealistic or exact recreations of upstream demos.

English and Arabic are selectable. Use the configured free neural voice client
or supplied audio; it sends narration text to Microsoft's online speech service.
If unavailable, report the failure and choose another available free/local engine
without pretending a system voice is neural AI. Align scenes and captions to
generated audio. Never pad a short script with meaningless visuals to meet duration;
develop meaningful action, or ask for more story when necessary.

Use free licensed assets where they help, or create local geometry. Keep provenance
and licenses. Inspect contact sheets, phone frames, acting and causal transitions,
fix issues, and generate a distinct designed cover. Numerical checks do not count
as listening or editorial review. Keep draft status until actually reviewed.

For the original Arabic memory story's independent collage, Clawd brush and lit
3D paper-book treatments, use docs/REFERENCE_AND_STORY_EDITS.md and the dedicated
memory-story command. For the supplied fox/kitten animation direction, use the
warm-creature-story skill and docs/WARM_CREATURE_STORIES.md. Its keyframe and
native generator workflow require real animation before an episode can be exported.
